# Vesla (`veslapack.com`) — Cosmetic Glass Packaging Manufacturer

Official web platform for **Vesla Co., Ltd.** ([https://www.veslapack.com](https://www.veslapack.com)), a Guangzhou-based cosmetic glass packaging manufacturer specializing in low-MOQ OEM/ODM production and door-to-door DDP global logistics for indie and scaling beauty brands.

## Core Capabilities

- **Low MOQ Manufacturing**: 5,000 pcs on stock molds with custom Pantone finishes; 10,000 pcs for private custom molds.
- **In-House Decoration**: Acid frosting, spray & gradient coating, silk-screen printing, hot stamping, electroplating, and water-transfer printing.
- **Quality & Compliance**: ISO 9001:2015, FDA 21 CFR, EU 1935/2004, REACH & RoHS compliant; AQL 1.5/2.5 inspection and ISTA-3A drop-tested export cartons.
- **Global DDP Logistics**: 20–26 days door-to-door sea/trucking to the USA & EU (customs & duties paid), plus 5–7 day DHL Express sample kits.
